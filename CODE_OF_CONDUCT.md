# Community conduct

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Critique the work, not the person. Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, and publishing private information are not acceptable.

Use public issues for reproducible product problems and bounded proposals. Keep customer information, secrets, and security-sensitive details out of public discussion.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and restrict participation to protect contributors and users. Decisions should be proportionate and explained when doing so does not reveal private information.

For a conduct issue requiring privacy, request a private contact route without posting the sensitive material publicly. Security issues should follow SECURITY.md.
